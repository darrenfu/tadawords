# Third-party notices

## Twemoji graphics

Concrete-word picture hints use small Twemoji PNG assets downloaded on demand
from the pinned `jdecked/twemoji` 17.0.3 release and cached privately on the
device.

Copyright X Corp. and other contributors. Graphics are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The source project is [jdecked/twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji).

Tada Words does not send a child name, profile identifier, learning history,
or typed word to the CDN. It requests only a fixed Unicode asset filename from
the app's child-safe concrete-word catalog.
