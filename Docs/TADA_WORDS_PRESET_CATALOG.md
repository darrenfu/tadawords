## Method and sources

The catalog is an original Tada Words selection and arrangement. Educational references guide age/skill scope; no branded commercial word list is copied wholesale.

- [Tada Words Original Editorial Catalog](https://github.com/darrenfu/tadawords) — All list membership, ordering, category boundaries, and child-facing summaries are an independent Tada Words editorial selection. The catalog does not reproduce or claim to be any branded commercial word list.
- [IES What Works Clearinghouse: Foundational Skills to Support Reading for Understanding in Kindergarten Through 3rd Grade](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/21/Published) — Used only for the evidence-based progression from sound awareness and decoding toward word recognition, vocabulary, and reading connected text; it is not used as a word-list source.
- [Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework: Language for Preschoolers](https://www.headstart.gov/school-readiness/article/language-preschool) — Used as developmental scope for preschool vocabulary involving actions, emotions, things, ideas, categories, and everyday conversation; actual words are independently curated.
- [University of Florida Literacy Institute: UFLI Foundations](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/) — Used only to validate a broad phonics progression such as short vowels, digraphs, blends, silent e, vowel teams, and word endings. No proprietary lesson list or sequence is copied.
- [Common Core State Standards: English Language Arts Standards](https://corestandards.org/english-language-arts-standards/) — Used as grade-band scope for print concepts, phonics, word recognition, vocabulary, language categories, and morphology. The standards do not prescribe this catalog's word membership.
- [Princeton WordNet](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/) — Used as a lexical reference when checking ordinary English meanings and semantic grouping; category choices and child suitability remain Tada Words editorial decisions.
- [Wikidata Knowledge Base](https://www.wikidata.org/) — Used as a factual naming reference for entities such as animals, dinosaurs, vehicles, and countries, not as a source for educational sequencing or list ranking.
