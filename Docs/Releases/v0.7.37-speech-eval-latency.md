# Tada Words v0.7.37 — Faster Read Evaluation

- Build: `2026072411`
- Issue: `#122`
- Batch: `speech-eval-latency-v0.7.37`

## Change

Read practice no longer adds a second wait for Apple's final recognition
callback after a usable partial result has already stabilized. A
high-confidence exact or pronunciation-equivalent target partial completes
immediately. Other usable partials settle after a 450 ms stability window.

## Safety boundaries

- Low-confidence target text cannot take the immediate-match path.
- Confident non-target speech still evaluates through the normal decision
  policy.
- Final callback, timeout, cancellation, speaker-filter, and technical-failure
  paths remain available.
- The change does not alter microphone permissions, voiceprint policy,
  production identity, CloudKit, or persistent child data.

## Acceptance

- Focused Apple speech adapter tests.
- Full source checks and iPhone/iPad simulator builds on the immutable PR HEAD.
- Data-preserving signed LocalQA install and launch on one iPhone and one iPad.
- Human Read-practice timing acceptance on the physical iPhone.
